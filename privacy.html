Privacy Policy

What happens to your details when you use this website or get in touch.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Tom Wareham, a sole trader trading as Tom Wareham Construction, based in Monmouth, Wales. Tom is the data controller for any personal information collected through this site, which means he decides how it is used and is responsible for looking after it.

If you have any questions about this policy or your information, contact Tom directly:

  • Email: Thomaswareham93@gmail.com
  • Phone: 07379 578 082

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through the contact form, we collect:

  • your name
  • your phone number
  • your email address, if you choose to give it
  • your postcode
  • the description of your job, plus anything else you include in your message

If you phone, text or email instead, we keep the details you give us in that conversation. If you go on to have work done, we will also hold the information needed to carry out the job, such as the site address, quotes, invoices and payment records.

If you accept analytics cookies, we also collect information about how you use the website. This is covered in the cookies section below.

How we use it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry, arrange a visit, prepare a quote and, if you go ahead, carry out and invoice the work. We use your postcode to check the job is within the area we cover.

We will not send you marketing, and we never sell or rent your information to anyone.

Our legal basis

UK data protection law requires us to have a lawful reason for using your information. Ours are:

  • Steps before a contract. When you ask for a quote, we need your details to respond and to price the work.
  • Performing a contract. If you hire us, we need your details to do the job.
  • Legal obligation. We have to keep financial records for tax purposes.
  • Consent. We only use analytics cookies if you agree to them, and you can change your mind at any time.

Who we share it with

We only share your information with services that help us run the business, and only as far as they need it:

  • Formspree receives and stores contact form submissions and forwards them to Tom by email.
  • Google provides Tom's email account, and Google Analytics if you accept analytics cookies. The fonts on this site are also loaded from Google, which means your browser shares your IP address with Google when the page loads.
  • Netlify hosts this website and keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.
  • Our accountant may see invoice and payment records for customers who have had work done.

Where we bring in a specialist trade for part of your job, we will pass on the details they need to do their part, such as your name, phone number and the site address.

Some of these providers are based in the United States or store data there. Where that happens, the transfer is protected by the safeguards UK law requires, such as the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't go ahead are deleted within 12 months.
  • Customer records, including quotes, invoices and payments, are kept for 6 years after the job finishes, because HMRC requires it.
  • Analytics data is kept by Google Analytics for 14 months.

Cookies

Cookies are small files that a website saves in your browser. This site uses two kinds.

Essential cookies remember whether you have accepted or rejected analytics cookies, so we don't ask you on every page. These don't identify you and can't be switched off, because the site needs them to respect your choice.

Analytics cookies are set by Google Analytics, through Google Tag Manager, and only if you click accept. They tell us which pages people visit, how they found the site and roughly where they are in the country, which helps us improve the site. They do not tell us who you are.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong
  • ask us to delete your information, unless we have to keep it by law
  • object to, or ask us to limit, how we use it
  • withdraw your consent to analytics cookies

To use any of these rights, email or phone Tom using the details above. We will respond within one month and there is no charge.

Keeping your information safe

Your details are kept in password-protected accounts and on Tom's own devices, and are only seen by Tom and anyone working on your job.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
